Register — {{ start_date }} to {{ end_date }}

The pharmacist signs twice — column 4 for the issue and column 13 for the return — and both print empty for a wet signature, as do the recipient's and the two officers'.
This register writes itself from who is logged in: when the pharmacist logs out, whoever logs in next is recorded as holding the key until he takes it back. One issue covers the whole absence; anybody else who logs in while it is out is noted against that same row as also on duty, rather than splitting it. Nobody has to fill a form in.
The app records the names, the times and who was logged in; it does not record a signature it never saw.
